Version: 0.1.5
Date: 2026-10-15
  Features:
    - Automatically present coherent monitor snapshots without changing existing drawing programs.
    - Support every complete monitor rectangle up to 6 by 6 tiles, all scales and cardinal orientations.
    - Add peripheral.setMonitorSyncGroup and getMonitorSyncGroup for up to four logical displays.
    - Include a bounded shared-phase sine-trace example and preserve the old four-row example as a baseline.
  Changes:
    - Stage replacements invisibly, publish complete groups together, and coalesce newer pending updates.
    - Keep model reads synchronous, successful earlier writes intact, and drawing rights shared.
    - Use demand-driven render headroom and incremental topology initialization under unchanged shared quotas.
    - Reevaluate old walls on upgrade; geometry-changing merges clear content and detach absorbed names.
  Info:
    - ALPHA: back up saves before upgrading. Rollback requires the pre-upgrade save and matching old mod.
    - Graphical and multiplayer-client validation is deferred to user review, not reported as passed.
